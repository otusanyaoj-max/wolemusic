About Wolemusic

Your gadget shop at Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife.

Who we are

Good gadgets at fair prices, close to campus.

Wolemusic helps students, lecturers and workers around OAU get the laptops, gadgets and accessories they need, without the stress of travelling to Lagos or Ibadan.

We check every item before it leaves our shop, explain what you're buying in plain words, and stay reachable on WhatsApp after you buy.
